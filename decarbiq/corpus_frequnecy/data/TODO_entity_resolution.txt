ENTITY RESOLUTION — Future Step (2.5)
======================================
Date noted: 2026-02-24

PROBLEM
-------
The pipeline currently matches claims by exact company_name string.
If one filing refers to "Consumers Energy" and another to "Consumers Power Company",
their claims are never compared — they live in completely separate silos.

Meanwhile, the LLM already extracts claims like:
  "Consumers Energy Company conducts business under the following assumed names:
   Consumers Business Energy Services, Consumers Energy, Consumers Energy Business
   Services, Consumers Energy Consultants, Consumers Energy Contractor Network,
   Consumers Energy Dealer Network, Consumers Energy Finance, Consumers Energy
   Fitness Audits, Consumers Energy Group, Consumers Energy HouseCall, Consumers
   Energy HouseCall Services, Consumers Energy Management, Consumers Energy
   Resources, Consumers Energy Security Services, Consumers Energy Services,
   Consumers Energy Systems, Consumers Energy Traders, Consumers Power, and
   Consumers Power Company."

This intelligence is captured as claim_text but never parsed into a lookup table.

WHAT TO BUILD
-------------
A new step (between step 2 and step 3, or post-step 3) that:

1. Scans extracted claims for alias/DBA/subsidiary patterns:
   - "conducts business under", "also known as", "d/b/a", "formerly known as",
     "doing business as", "a subsidiary of", "a wholly-owned subsidiary"

2. Builds a `company_aliases` table:
     canonical_name  TEXT    -- the primary/parent name
     alias_name      TEXT    -- the variant
     relationship    TEXT    -- dba | subsidiary | former_name | abbreviation
     source_id       INTEGER -- where we learned this

3. Uses fuzzy matching (Levenshtein, token overlap) to catch near-duplicates:
   - "CF Industries Holdings" vs "CF Industries Holdings, Inc."
   - "air products" vs "Air Products and Chemicals" vs "Air Products & Chemicals"

4. Step 5 then joins through the alias table instead of exact company_name match,
   so contradictions across name variants are properly detected.

AFFECTED FILES
--------------
- step2_schema.py   — add company_aliases table
- step3_read_pass.py or new step — parse alias claims into the table
- step5_contradiction.py — use canonical_name for grouping instead of raw company_name
