Title: Providing Access to Electricity to 300 Million People in Sub-Saharan Africa
URL: https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/video/2024/09/23/mission-300-providing-access-to-electricity-to-300-million-people-in-sub-saharan-africa-by-2030
Date: 2026-01-28
======================================================================

Roughly 600 million people in Sub-Saharan Africa lack access to electricity. Energy access is a lifeline for people, helping drive economic opportunities, boosting job creation, and improving education and health services. The World Bank Group, together with the African Development Bank, has committed to providing access to electricity to 300 million people in Sub-Saharan Africa by 2030. Known as “Mission 300,” this ambitious plan is attracting widespread support. Watch this video and join our ambitious mission to power up Africa and unlock its full potential.
