The European Hydrogen Regulatory Forum, organised by the European Commission, gathers key stakeholders across the European energy sector to discuss opportunities and challenges related to the development of the hydrogen market and regulation in Europe.
Completing the landscape of existing regulatory fora for gas and electricity, the first edition of the Hydrogen Regulatory Forum will take place in Rotterdam, and will be hosted by the Dutch Ministry of Climate Policy and Green Growth in coordination with the Dutch energy regulator.
The invited participants and speakers include EU countries, national regulatory authorities, the European Commission, representatives of the hydrogen sector including infrastructure operators, producers, end-users, appliances manufacturers, traders, as well as academia and NGOs.
Attendance at the Forum is on an invitation-only basis.
