Living our purpose
Wood Mackenzie’s purpose—to transform the way we power our planet—guides our work with Let’s Share the Sun, creating meaningful, lasting change in regions where energy access is critical. By contributing our expertise to support Let’s Share the Sun’s mission, providing access to clean energy in areas with unreliable or poor grid access, we are driving sustainable change and giving back to communities that need it most.
