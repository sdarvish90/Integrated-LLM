About the Portfolio
The Digital Mining Investment Portfolio, is a strategic toolkit developed by
InvestChile
in collaboration with the
Ministry
 of Mining
and the Chilean Copper Commission -
Cochilco
, to support the promotion of the mining sector in Chile at a global level.
The portfolio allows for quick access to strategic information related to current and future mining projects across the country, including minerals such as copper, lithium, gold, and more.

Estimated investment amounts

Technical specifications
Development stages
