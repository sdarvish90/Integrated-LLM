Accelerating Clean, Efficient, and Equitable Transportation for All.
Watch
We Must
Electrify all the ways in which people and goods move, including personal vehicles, two- and three-wheelers, public transportation, and fleets
Build robust, reliable, and accessible EV charging infrastructure
Create a circular, resilient, and responsible EV battery economy, one in which end-of-life batteries are reused, repurposed, and recycled
RMI Will
Provide robust data analysis, actionable insights, and implementation support to those involved in transportation electrification, including:
Local and national governments, who can accelerate electrification through well-informed policy and regulations
Fleets, who can greatly reduce emissions by electrifying the way they move goods
Industry, whose work with the public sector will play a crucial role in electrification
Utilities, who will be spearheading the critical grid improvements electrification requires
Affected communities, especially those most affected by transportation-related emissions and who have the most to gain from electrification
RMI is modernizing the transportation sector to  slash household spending, improve access to high-quality travel options, and save millions of lives.
