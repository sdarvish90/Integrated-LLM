Compliance Reports

Ancillary Services trades that result in a QSE’s purchased quantities of Ancillary Services exceeding the QSE’s Self-Arranged Ancillary Service Quantities. This product is delivered after the 1430 snapshot taken daily for the day-ahead.

This report displays ERCOT interconnection combined frequency response performance.

Frequency Measurable Events in ERCOT Interconnection

Disclosure Reports and Extracts
2-Day DAM Ancillary Services Reports
This report will contain all 48 Hour disclosure data related to DAM. The following individual files are included in the report:  2-Day Aggregate DAM AS Offers, 2-Day Cleared DAM AS, and 2-Day Self-Arranged AS.
2-Day DAM Bids and Offers Reports
This report will contain all 48 Hour disclosure data related to DAM. The following individual files are included in the report:  2-Day Total Cleared Energy Bids and Energy-Only Offers, 2-Day Total Cleared Energy Bids and Energy-Only Offers by Disclosure Areas, 2-Day Point-to-Point Obligation Bids, 2-Day Point-to-Point Obligation Bids by Disclosure Areas.
2-Day DAM Energy Curves
This report contains 2-Day aggregate energy demand and supply curve disclosure data for the Day-Ahead Market (DAM).  The following file types are included in this report:  Energy Demand Curves, Energy Supply Curves, and Minimum Energy Supply Curves, including by Disclosure Area.
2-Day Real-Time Gen and Load Data Reports
This report contains 2-Day aggregate disclosure data related to Real Time. The following file types are included in this report: Aggregate Generation Summary, Aggregate Load Summary, and Aggregate Output Schedules, including by Disclosure Area.
2-Day SCED Energy Curves
This report contains 2-Day aggregate energy demand and supply curve disclosure data for SCED. The following file types are included in this report: Energy Demand Curves for CLRs including by Disclosure Area, Supply Curve for ESR, Photovoltaic, Wind, and Non-Intermittent Renewable Resources, including by Disclosure Area.
3-Day DAM Highest Price AS Offer Selected
This report provides the offer price and the name of the Entity submitting the offer for the highest-priced Ancillary Service (AS) Offer selected in the Day-Ahead Market (DAM) for each AS three days after the end of the applicable Operating Day. If multiple Entities submitted the highest-priced offers selected, all Entities shall be identified.
3-Day Highest Price Bids Selected or Dispatched in SCED
The bid price and the name of the Entity submitting the bid for the highest-priced bid selected or Dispatched by SCED three days after the applicable Operating Day. If multiple Entities submitted the highest-priced bids selected, all Entities shall be identified on the MIS Public Area.
3-Day Highest Price Offered in SCED
ERCOT shall post the offer price and the name of the Entity submitting the offer for the highest-priced offer selected or Dispatched by SCED three days after the applicable Operating Day. If multiple Entities submitted the highest-priced offers selected, all Entities shall be identified on the MIS Public Area.
60-Day COP All Updates
This report displays all iterative COP submissions where a change has occurred for the Operating Day.

7-Day Event Trigger Posting
Seven days after the applicable Operating Day, ERCOT shall post the portion of any Resource's Energy Offer Curve or Energy Bid/Offer Curve that is at or above 50 times the Fuel Index Price (50xFIP) for any Security-Constrained Economic Dispatch (SCED) interval in the Real-Time Market (RTM) where any Real-Time Locational Marginal Price (LMP) exceeds 50xFIP, or the portion of any Resource’s Ancillary Service Offer that is at or above 50xFIP for any operating Hour in Day-Ahead Market (DAM) or any SCED intervals in Real-Time Market (RTM) where any Ancillary Service Market Clearing Price for Capacity (MCPC) exceeds 50xFIP.
