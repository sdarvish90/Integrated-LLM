Opinion & analysis guidelines
We actively encourage industry experts and companies to share their knowledge with our readers in the form of guest articles. If you wish to submit such an article, please adhere to the following guidelines:
The article should be written exclusively for
pv magazine
, and address a relevant industry issue, which is aimed at a technical B2B audience
Avoid using “marketing speak” – the article should be written from an unbiased POV, as much as possible, and not be promotional
Back up any assertions with evidence and appropriate sources
The article should be no longer than 10,000 characters (inc. spaces)
Please include a head shot and short bio of the author, and relevant images in high res
