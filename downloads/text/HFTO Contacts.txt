The Hydrogen and Fuel Cell Technologies Office (HFTO) conducts comprehensive efforts to overcome the technological, economic, and institutional barriers to the development of hydrogen and fuel cells. HFTO is within the Office of Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy aligned with the strategic vision and goals of the U.S. Department of Energy.
