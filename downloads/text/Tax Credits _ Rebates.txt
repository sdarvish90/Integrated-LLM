Power Your Home With Rebates
Through the Home Energy Rebates, you may be eligible for cash back on appliances and other home improvements that can lower your energy bills. Your state, territory, or Tribe manages rebates for energy efficiency and appliance upgrades. They will determine which products are eligible for rebates.
