Our guide to the most influential people in the global wind industry
Welcome to Windpower Monthly’s first ever wind industry power list. The list spans the most senior people in the global wind industry and was compiled by Windpower Monthly’s editorial team. The inaugural list, spanning Europe, Asia and the Americas, features almost 100 wind industry leaders, from turbine firms, developers and major wind associations, as well as investors and key people from the wider wind supply chain.
It is intended as a snapshot of the most powerful people in the wind sector and was correct at the time of publication in June 2025. The editorial team will update the list every year with the wind power leaders of the future and we invite you to contact the team to suggest new entrants for us to consider.
Enter a name, company or job title in the search bar above to see if they are included in the 2025 list or simply scroll through the individual entries below and click on the ones you are interested in for a biography of the person listed.
We hope you enjoy the 2025 wind power list!
Ian Griggs, Editor, Windpower Monthly
