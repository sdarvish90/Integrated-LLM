Global Center of Excellence in Drone and Geospatial Technologies

Drone Powered Solutions

PwC Drone Powered Solutions – Global Center of Excellence in Drone and Geospatial Technologies - is a pioneering consulting group dedicated exclusively to implementing drone and satellite technologies for commercial, international development, alongside other security, defence and public safety applications.

Being well recognised as global leaders in the drone and satellite industries, our team offers:

  • Strong experience in identifying and assessing disruptive and transformative growth areas, including what is needed for success
  • Fresh perspective on the drone and AAM market with our unique understanding of market needs, drivers and enablers, as well as insights on potentials and barriers
  • Global scale of presence and scope of activity to give strategic guidance in drone and AAM market equipped with tailored business strategies

Our core offering

Regulatory advisory

Provision of insights to institutions on drone regulations and review of technology-related projects; development of regulatory frameworks along with implementation roadmaps tailored to each country’s specifications.

 

Government strategies

Development of comprehensive governmental strategies to facilitate safe and secure drone operations, foster the digitalisation of related processes, increase public awareness about regulatory compliance, and showcase the benefits presented by technology.

 

New products and services

Offering of new products and services that could be carried out with the use of drones for quicker and more secure operations across different industrial sectors.

 

Market analysis and sizing

Conducting market analysis and sizing of drone services and ecosystems, as well as assessing the market potential for innovative drone solutions to replace more traditional methods. This involves considering the maturity of the use case, technical feasibility, and commercial justification.

 

Pricing strategies

Development of pricing models and strategies for drone-related services that will have a positive impact on industry growth.

Strategic Advisory

Regulatory advisory; Government strategies; New products and services; Market analysis and sizing; Pricing strategies

Scaling drone programmes

Consulting services on the design of comprehensive drone programmes and support throughout their implementation process, as well as sustainable development in the future

 

Technology assessment

Advisory on the applicability and range of benefits presented by drone and satellite technologies in diverse contexts

 

Commercial and technical DD

Consulting services on commercial and technical due diligence processes that allow for precise decision-making in a reduced period of time

 

Data governance

Provision of advisory services on the principles and practices of data governance to ensure data security, privacy, accuracy, and usability

 

Operating models and processes

Consulting services on the development of operating models and processes tailored specifically to project needs and requirements

 

Technology Consulting

Scaling drone programmes; Technology assessment; Commercial and technical DD; Data governance; Operating models and processes

Pilot projects

Consulting services and detailed analysis of the most suitable approaches and practices when launching drone pilot projects; comprehensive support and guidance on drone technology integration into operational processes during the implementation phase

 

Large scale drone and satellite data projects

Development and implementation of drone and satellite data projects on national and regional levels; in-depth analysis of existing challenges and identification of the most suitable solutions to increase automation, provide impact, and ensure cost savings.

 

PwC Construction Insights

Provision of satellite and drone-powered solutions to overcome challenges frequently connected to a lack of effective capacity to supervise large area sites; possibility to track a large number of KPIs related to construction progress and land utilisation

 

Remote Asset Insights solutions

Remote Asset Insights foresee a more sustainable and innovative way to digitise and enhance the site visit experience. Through the combination of satellite, drone, and video data, the solution delivers a fully digital inspection of a facility and related data analytics, substituting in-person visits

 

PMO

Provision of comprehensive management support and coordination from project initiation to completion, ensuring seamless execution at every stage

 

Operations and Managed Services

Pilot projects; Large scale drone and satellite data projects; PwC Construction Insights; Remote Asset Insights solutions; PMO

PwC Geospatial.App software

PwC Geospatial.App is a cloud-based drone and satellite data visualization, analytics, and management platform. The easy-to-use interface allows users to engage based on their specific needs, including assessment against detailed construction or site plans, progress reports, and other asset-related data

 

Photogrammetry

Provision of photogrammetry and image data analytics giving rise to comprehensive information not available before

 

AI and Machine Learning

Development of advanced Machine Learning models, along with the creation of advisory strategies on how to integrate innovative drone technology practices based on technologies such as drones and AI into operational processes and corporate culture for specific industries

 

Drone and satellite data analytics

Services for drone and satellite data analytics aimed at enhancing automation and offering comprehensive insights, enabling informed decision-making for a wide range of industries

 

Custom solutions development

Considering the project’s specifications and requirements, development of custom solutions is proposed to automate the analysis of data and support the decision-making process

Data Analytics

PwC Geospatial.App software; Photogrammetry; AI and Machine Learning; Drone and satellite data analytics; Custom solutions development
